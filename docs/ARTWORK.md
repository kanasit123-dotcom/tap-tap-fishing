# Artwork Provenance

All shipped artwork was generated by the user in ChatGPT on 2026-10-05 from the prompts in
[ART-PROMPTS.md](ART-PROMPTS.md) (semi-realistic painted style, true RGBA sheets for creatures and boat, opaque seas)
and processed into game assets with `tools/sprites.py`. Sheet H (12 extra species) was drawn smaller inside its cells, so those sprites
are 136-249 px wide; the big ones (whale shark, barracuda) are slightly upscaled on large iPads. No UNIS game files, screenshots or cabinet artwork were used,
and nothing was imported from the sibling repositories. Treasure Cove was only a gameplay reference.

The earlier cartoon set (`cove.png`, `sea-creatures.png`, `deep-creatures.png`, generated 2026-10-04 with Codex's built-in
image tool) was replaced by this one and removed in the commit after `ab5e4cc`; it remains available in Git history.

## Processed output (published)

- `public/assets/sprites/<id>.webp` for each of the 46 catalog entries plus `boat.webp` (WebP with alpha, quality 90;
  longest side 420-900 px, 8-109 KB each).
- `public/assets/sea-portrait.webp` (1024x1536) and `sea-landscape.webp` (1536x1024).
- Total about 1.2 MB. Sizes and anchors are recorded in `src/art-manifest.js`, which the tool rewrites on every run.
- Cutting: connected-component detection on a 4x downscaled alpha mask, grown by 20 px so thin parts stay attached
  (butterflyfish streamer, swordfish bill, ray tail, lobster antennae, squid tentacles, anglerfish lure), then trimmed with
  a 6 px margin. `art/preview/*.png` (git-ignored) shows every piece on a checkerboard for inspection.
- Boat anchors, measured on `boat.png`: rod-holder opening at (1350, 462) and the white/red hull boundary at y=706.
  Background lines: portrait waterline 0.126 / sand 0.775, landscape 0.182 / 0.806 (fractions of image height).
  They live in `ANCHORS` in `tools/sprites.py`; remeasure them if the boat or seas are regenerated.
- Home-screen icons (`public/icons/`) are drawn by `tools/make_icon.py` from the clownfish sprite and the portrait sea.
- The rod, line, hook, sinker, bubbles, light rays, plankton, splashes and coin bursts are drawn by code in `src/scene.js`.

## Prompts

The exact prompts are in [ART-PROMPTS.md](ART-PROMPTS.md) (sections A-G). Regenerating any sheet means saving it under the
same file name in `art/incoming/` and running `python tools/sprites.py <sheet-name>`.
